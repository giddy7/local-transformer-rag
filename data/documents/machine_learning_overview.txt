Machine Learning (ML) is a subfield of artificial intelligence that focuses on building algorithms that learn patterns from data. Instead of being explicitly programmed with static rules, ML algorithms construct statistical models from sample training data. Supervised learning algorithms build predictive models from labeled datasets with known target outputs. Unsupervised learning discovers hidden patterns, clusters, and representations in unlabeled data without target labels. Reinforcement learning trains autonomous agents to make sequences of decisions by maximizing cumulative rewards in an environment.
