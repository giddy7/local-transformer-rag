Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) is an architectural framework that enhances Large Language Models by retrieving relevant documents from an external knowledge base. A standard RAG pipeline consists of document ingestion, text chunking, dense vector embedding, similarity search in a vector database, and prompt-conditioned answer generation. RAG significantly reduces model hallucination, ensures grounding in authoritative source documents, and provides verifiable citations. Dense retrievers use dual encoder neural networks trained with InfoNCE contrastive loss to map questions and passages into a shared vector space.
