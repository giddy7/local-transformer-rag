The Transformer architecture was introduced in the seminal 2017 paper 'Attention Is All You Need' by Vaswani et al. Transformers replace recurrent neural networks (RNNs) and LSTM networks with multi-head self-attention mechanisms. This enables parallel computation across all tokens in a sequence, eliminating the sequential bottleneck of recurrence. Key components include Scaled Dot-Product Attention, Sinusoidal Positional Encodings, Feed-Forward Networks, and Residual Layer Normalization. Transformers form the foundational backbone for modern Large Language Models, BERT, GPT, and Retrieval-Augmented Generation systems.
